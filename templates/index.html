Frequently Asked Questions

Is ResumeCraft completely free to use?

Yes! ResumeCraft is 100% free and open-source. You can build, customize with 5 templates, run AI optimization diagnostics, and download unlimited PDF resumes with zero hidden costs or subscriptions.

Are the resume templates Applicant Tracking System (ATS) friendly?

Yes. All templates follow strict ATS formatting guidelines: standard section titles (Education, Experience, Projects, Skills), clean hierarchical fonts, standard date formats, and machine-readable text structures that parse smoothly in ATS scanners like Greenhouse, Lever, and Workday.

How does the built-in AI ATS Score Checker work?

The AI diagnostics engine audits your resume across essential categories: contact information completeness, summary impact, bullet point action verbs and metrics (percentages, numbers), technical skill count, and section coverage to give you a dynamic 0–100% grade and actionable tips.

How do I save and load my resume drafts?

Click the Save Resume button in the top navigation bar to persist your draft into the database. You can manage, reload, or delete saved versions at any time by clicking My Resumes.
